# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) - IBADAT International University, Islamabad

Source: https://iiui.edu.pk/faqs/

## General FAQs

### Q1: What degree programs are offered at IBADAT International University?
IBADAT International University offers 4-year and 5-year undergraduate degree programs (BS CS, BS SE, BS AI, BS Civil Engineering, Pharm-D, DPT, BBA, BSAF, LL.B, BS MLT, BS Nursing), 2-year Associate Degree Programs (ADP), 2-year MS/M.Phil degree programs, and PhD programs.

### Q2: Is IBADAT International University recognized by HEC and professional councils?
Yes. IBADAT International University, Islamabad is fully chartered by the Government and recognized by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan. Its professional programs are accredited by respective councils including the Pharmacy Council of Pakistan (PCP), Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC), and Pakistan Nursing Council (PNC).

### Q3: How do I submit an application for admission?
You can apply online by visiting the official admissions portal at https://iiui.edu.pk/apply or by visiting the University Admissions Office on campus.

### Q4: Are scholarships or financial aid options available?
Yes. IBADAT International University provides two types of financial support:
1. **Merit-Based Scholarships**: Up to 100% tuition fee waiver for top position holders in board/university examinations.
2. **Need-Based Financial Support**: Fee concessions for deserving candidates based on income verification and panel interviews.

### Q5: Is there any entry test for admission?
Yes. The university conducts the IBADAT International University Entrance Test. Standardized test scores such as NAT, HAT, ECAT, or LAT (for LL.B) are also accepted.

### Q6: Are hostel facilities available for outstation students?
Yes. Separate hostels are available for male and female students near campus. Dedicated university bus transport is provided for daily commuting.

### Q7: Are there any quota seats reserved for specific provinces or backgrounds?
No. Admissions are strictly on Open Merit. There are no provincial or background quotas.

### Q8: What is the fee refund policy of the university?
As per Higher Education Commission (HEC) National Fee Refund Policy:
- **100% Tuition Fee Refund**: Up to 7th day of commencement of classes.
- **50% Tuition Fee Refund**: From 8th to 15th day of commencement of classes.
- **No Refund**: After the 15th day of commencement of classes.
- *Note: Admission fee is non-refundable.*

### Q9: Is co-education permitted on campus?
Yes. IBADAT International University provides equal learning opportunities to both male and female students.
