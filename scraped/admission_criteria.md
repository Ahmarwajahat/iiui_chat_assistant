# Admission Criteria & Eligibility - IBADAT International University, Islamabad

Source: https://iiui.edu.pk/admission-criteria/

## General Admission Policy
Admissions at IBADAT International University (IIUI), Islamabad are conducted twice a year under the semester system (Fall and Spring Semesters). 

- **Merit System**: All admissions are granted strictly on Open Merit. There are no quota seats or discrimination based on ethnicity, region, religion, or gender.
- **Selection Process**: Final merit is calculated based on past academic record (Matriculation/Intermediate), entrance test score, and interview performance where applicable.
- **Provisional Admission**: Students awaiting final 2nd-year Intermediate (F.Sc/FA/ICS/I.Com/A-Level) results may apply for provisional admission. Admitted students must submit official mark sheets upon result declaration.
- **Equivalence**: Candidates with foreign qualifications (e.g., Cambridge A-Levels/IB) must submit an IBCC Equivalence Certificate.

## Faculty-Specific Eligibility Requirements

### 1. Faculty of Computer Sciences
* **BS Computer Science (BS CS) - 4 Years (8 Semesters)**
  - **Eligibility**: Minimum 50% marks in Intermediate (HSSC) / F.Sc (Pre-Engineering, Pre-Medical, ICS, I.Com, A-Levels, or DAE Computer Science).
  - **Pre-Medical Deficiency Rule**: Candidates with F.Sc Pre-Medical (minimum 50% marks) are eligible but must pass 6 credit hours of deficiency Mathematics courses within the first year as per NCEAC/HEC rules.
  - **Entry Test**: IIUI Entry Test or valid NAT score.

* **BS Software Engineering (BS SE) - 4 Years (8 Semesters)**
  - **Eligibility**: Minimum 50% marks in Intermediate / F.Sc (Pre-Engineering, ICS, I.Com, A-Level, DAE) with Mathematics.
  - **Pre-Medical Deficiency Rule**: Eligible with 50% marks, subject to clearing 6 credit hours of deficiency Mathematics.
  - **Entry Test**: IIUI Entry Test or valid NAT score.

* **BS Artificial Intelligence (BS AI) - 4 Years (8 Semesters)**
  - **Eligibility**: Minimum 50% marks in F.Sc Pre-Engineering / ICS / Pre-Medical (with deficiency math).
  - **Entry Test**: IIUI Entry Test or valid NAT score.

* **MS Computer Science (MS CS) - 2 Years (4 Semesters)**
  - **Eligibility**: 16 years of education (BS CS/SE/IT or equivalent) with minimum 2.5/4.0 CGPA or 60% marks.
  - **Entry Test**: GAT General or IIUI Graduate Entry Test.

### 2. Faculty of Engineering & Technology
* **BS Civil Engineering - 4 Years (8 Semesters)**
  - **Eligibility**: Minimum 60% marks in HSSC / F.Sc Pre-Engineering or equivalent DAE in Civil Technology.
  - **Entry Test**: IIUI Entry Test, UET Entry Test, or ECAT.

* **MS Civil Engineering - 2 Years (4 Semesters)**
  - **Eligibility**: 16 years of education (BS Civil Engineering) with minimum 2.5/4.0 CGPA + PEC Registration.

---

### 3. Faculty of Pharmacy
* **Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm-D) - 5 Years (10 Semesters)**
  - **Eligibility**: Minimum 60% marks in F.Sc Pre-Medical or equivalent IBCC certified qualification.
  - **Entry Test**: IIUI Pharmacy Entrance Test & Interview.

* **M.Phil Pharmacology / Pharmaceutics - 2 Years (4 Semesters)**
  - **Eligibility**: Pharm-D / B.Pharm degree with valid Pharmacy Council registration and minimum 2.5 CGPA.

---

### 4. Faculty of Allied Health & Biological Sciences
* **Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) - 5 Years (10 Semesters)**
  - **Eligibility**: Minimum 60% marks in F.Sc Pre-Medical (Biology, Physics, Chemistry) or A-Level equivalent.
  - **Entry Test**: IIUI Entrance Test.

* **BS Medical Lab Technology (BS MLT) - 4 Years (8 Semesters)**
  - **Eligibility**: Minimum 50% marks in F.Sc Pre-Medical or equivalent.

* **BS Human Nutrition & Dietetics - 4 Years (8 Semesters)**
  - **Eligibility**: Minimum 45% marks in F.Sc Pre-Medical or equivalent.

* **BS Biotechnology - 4 Years (8 Semesters)**
  - **Eligibility**: Minimum 45% marks in F.Sc Pre-Medical / Pre-Engineering / ICS.

* **BS Nursing - 4 Years (8 Semesters)**
  - **Eligibility**: Minimum 50% marks in F.Sc Pre-Medical.

---

### 5. Faculty of Social Sciences & Management
* **Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) - 4 Years (8 Semesters)**
  - **Eligibility**: Minimum 45% marks in F.A / F.Sc / I.Com / ICS / A-Level.

* **BS Accounting & Finance (BSAF) - 4 Years (8 Semesters)**
  - **Eligibility**: Minimum 45% marks in Intermediate or equivalent.

* **Bachelor of Laws (LL.B) - 5 Years (10 Semesters)**
  - **Eligibility**: Minimum 45% marks in Intermediate + valid HEC Law Admission Test (LAT) score of at least 50%.
